HOW TO PLAY

1. Listen
Read what the character actually says before replying.
2. Quick Replies
Use suggested responses or write your own.
3. Watch Stats
Stress, trust and connection react to your actual message.
4. Silence
After 10 seconds without sending a message, the character may panic.
5. XP
Good communication earns XP. Harmful or dismissive messages can lose XP.
6. Hint
Use the hint when you are unsure what kind of response would help.
7. Daily Scenario
A different scenario can be featured each day.
8. Previous Sessions
Your completed sessions and scores are saved locally.

Important: This is a fictional communication simulator. It does not replace real crisis or emergency support.
