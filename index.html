How To Play

1. Choose a character

Characters have different difficulty levels. Each scenario lasts 5 minutes.

2. Read carefully

Listen to what the person is saying. Your goal is to respond with empathy and support.

3. Keep the conversation natural

Ask questions, acknowledge feelings and avoid judging or dismissing what they say.

4. Watch distress

The distress meter changes based on your responses. Supportive responses can reduce distress.

5. Emergency situations

If the character appears to be in immediate danger, encourage them to stay with someone they trust and seek immediate professional or emergency help.

6. API key

Enter your OpenRouter API key in the game and press SAVE KEY. It is stored only in this browser's local storage.
